# Dev-009: Lonely fall and a new cast on every start

[Bugfix] [Feature] [Engine] [Data] [Client]

During the opening fall the narrator described Ivy next to Niko in the sky. Niko keeps his seed tile
inside the house while he falls, so `visibleActors` reported the NPCs that can see that tile, and the
narrator also received the house's "Living Room" as the place. Separately, "Start new game" replayed
the same two characters; it should bring a new cast every time.

## 1. Findings

- `visibleActors` has no notion of the fall: it measures line of sight from the seed tile.
- `narrate` sent `zone.name`, `zone.description` and the room even in the `fall` phase.
- `reset` reused `seedValue`, and `seed()` always read `data/npcs.json`.
- The scene facts and the agendas name Marta and Ivy, so a new cast also needs new wording for them.

## 2. Decisions

- During the fall nobody is perceptible and the place is "The sky"; the system prompt says so too.
- A cast is a pure function of `(cast.json, seed)` through `rngFor(seed, 0, "cast")`: no model, no
  cost, replayable. An LLM could propose casts later, validated by the same `loadCast` rules.
- `data/cast.json` holds name and temperament pools plus **role slots** (warner, finder). A slot owns
  its agenda and the scene fact that agenda reveals, with `{name}` placeholders and no pronouns.
- Fact ids keep their old names (`fact_marta_warning`) so existing saves keep their `facts_known`; the
  save stores the reworded texts in `settings.fact_texts` and `Engine.knownFacts` prefers them.
- `reset(onDelta, { seed })` makes a new world: the seed is drawn in `src/server.ts` (outside the
  simulation), stored in the save, and also seeds the street, the landing and wandering. A reset
  without a seed restarts the authored game, which keeps the tests stable.
- Niko stays: he is the protagonist, not part of the cast.
- The optional `cast` role (own `CAST_MODEL`/`CAST_REASONING`/`CAST_IDLE_MS`, reasoning off) writes the
  personality and voice of the generated characters. `reset` seeds the save first with the pool
  temperaments and asks the model afterwards, so the call is logged in the new save and a failure leaves a
  whole game. `parseCastDraft` drops unknown ids, missing fields and texts longer than 80/160 characters.

## 3. What must not break

- The engine is the only writer of state; the client still only draws `GET /api/state`.
- Determinism: the cast, the street and the landing come from the saved seed.
- No migration: `settings` already holds free keys, and a save without `fact_texts` reads as before.

## 4. Acceptance

- Nobody visible, the sky as the place and no room during the fall; the house returns on landing
  (`test/cast.test.ts`; confirmed to fail without the guard, where Ivy showed up as "near, north").
- `generateCast` is deterministic, distinct per seed and never repeats the authored names
  (`test/cast.test.ts`).
- A seeded reset replaces every NPC, the seed and the fact wording, and replays identically
  (`test/cast.test.ts`); an unseeded reset restores Marta and Ivy.
- A malformed `cast.json` is rejected on load (`test/cast.test.ts`).
- `parseCastDraft`, the writer (role `cast` logged, over budget makes no call) and `reset` (partial picks,
  a throwing writer, no call without a seed) are covered in `test/cast.test.ts`.
- Pressing "Start new game" shows a different cast: `manual`, it needs the browser.
- A real model writing personalities: `manual`, the OpenRouter roles are not reachable from the tests.
