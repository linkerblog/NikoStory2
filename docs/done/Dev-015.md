# Dev-015: A new start each game, characters that follow, and a larger city

[Feature] [Engine] [World] [Data]

Every new game opened in the same house, with the same two people standing where they always stood, and
once they had said their piece they froze in place. The street was one 40x40 block with six shops. This makes
the start a draw (a different home, in a different part of town), gives every character a bearing toward
Niko (some shadow him through doors, some drift behind, some only linger) and grows the city from one street
to five districts joined by edge exits.

## 1. Findings

- `data/niko.json` fixes `start_zone: house_001` and `data/opening.json` fixes the landing room. `seed()` plants
  Niko and every NPC there, and `generateOverland` in `src/zones.ts` hard-codes the house lot and the six buildings.
- An NPC in `Engine.stepWorld` acts only in Niko's zone. After its agenda is `arrived` or `done` it does
  nothing at all (`continue` before the wander routine), and a character left behind at a door never comes after
  Niko. `follow` exists as an agenda kind, but no data uses it and it stops at the zone edge.
- Items and the scene point at the house: `items.json` lays the letter and the key at fixed tiles of `house_001`,
  and the finder's agenda visits the object `table`.
- A door is a `D` tile with a portal; `entryTile` derives where a mover appears from the reciprocal portal. Nothing
  in that mechanism needs the other side to be a building, so a `D` on a map's border links two outdoor zones.

## 2. Decisions

- **The start is a draw of the seed.** `data/homes.json` holds four more homes next to the authored
  `house_001`. `pickStart` draws one home and one lot of the city (`rngFor(seed, 0, "start")`); the authored game
  (no explicit seed) keeps `house_001` on the street lot, byte for byte. The choice is stored in `settings`
  (`home`, `lot`) so a save replays and a restart reads it back.
- **A home is a contract.** Each one has a room `living_room`, an object `table` and one `wardrobe`, a free start
  tile, and spawn tiles for every cast role. `loadHomes` rejects a home that breaks it. `items.json` entries gain
  `on`: the item moves to that object of the chosen home, so the letter is always on the table and the key in the
  wardrobe.
- **Bearing is data, with a name.** `data/cast.json` defines `bearings` (`shadow`, `companion`, `trailing`, `shy`,
  `lingers`, `stays`), each with a mode, a range, a leash, a chance per tick and a phrase. Every temperament names
  one, the generated NPC stores the resolved object in `entities.data.bearing`, and the optional `cast` role may
  choose a name too; an unknown name falls back to the pool's.
- **Bearing steps are ordinary moves.** A character with a bearing that has finished its agenda (or only had to
  visit an object) walks toward Niko with the same `bfsStep`, `free` and `move` events as anyone, with a per-tick
  chance drawn from `rngFor`. `approach` agendas that were `arrived` resume when Niko walks away.
- **Following through a door is a trail, not a teleport.** When Niko uses a portal, each `follow` character of
  that zone within its leash gets `data.trail = { to, due }` with `due` = ticks to reach the door. When it falls
  due and Niko is still in `to`, the engine puts it on the tile next to the reciprocal door (a short ring search
  when Niko stands on it) and records an `enter` event. If Niko has moved on, the trail is dropped. No
  migration: the state is entity data, and `trail` is hidden from prompts like the other raw keys.
- **Responders and legacy NPCs are untouched.** Only a character with a `bearing` behaves this way; a responder
  has none, so incidents and their tests keep their meaning.
- **The city is data.** `data/city.json` holds the districts (size, buildings with a theme, home lots, edge exits,
  scenery, rooms). The street keeps its id `outdoor` and its layout; four districts hang off it: the market (north),
  the park (east), the riverside (west) and the station (south). `loadCity` validates every rectangle, door, exit
  and reciprocity, and flood-fills each district so every door can be reached.
- **Interiors know their street.** A generated building's exit returns to the district that holds it (`parent`
  and its label come from the zone Niko leaves) and picks its furniture from its theme.
- **Old saves.** `ensureWorld` adds the missing exits and districts to a stored street without touching
  anything else, so a save from 0.12.0 gains the new city on the next boot.

## 3. Changes

### 3.1 A different start

- `src/homes.ts` (new): `Home`, `loadHomes`, `pickStart`; `data/homes.json` (new).
- `src/world.ts` `seed`: takes the start; `src/cast.ts` `generateCast`: spawns from the home.
- `src/items.ts` `loadItems`: `on` resolves against the home; `data/items.json`: `on` for the letter and the key.
- `src/game.ts`: draws the start in `reset`, plants what it chose.

### 3.2 Bearing

- `src/cast.ts`: `Bearing`, `parseBearing`, `readBearing`; `bearings` and a `bearing` per temperament in
  `data/cast.json`; the `cast` role may name one; `src/world.ts` `applyPersonalities` keeps it.
- `src/engine.ts`: `bearingStep`, trails in `enterPortal` and `runTrails`, `arrived` approach resumes; `src/combat.ts`
  hides `trail`. `data/npcs.json`: bearings for Marta and Ivy. `src/npc.ts`: the prompt names the bearing.

### 3.3 The city

- `src/zones.ts`: `generateDistrict`, `ensureWorld` over `data/city.json`, exits for an old street, themed interiors.
- `src/engine.ts` `buildZone`/`enterPortal`: the parent zone of an interior; `entryTile` gains a ring search.
- `data/city.json` (new), `data/rules.json`: ambient counts stay keyed by kind.

## 4. What must not break

- The engine is the only writer of state; the interpreter and the `npc` role only propose. A bearing is a move
  the engine validates with `free`, never a shortcut through a wall.
- Determinism: every draw goes through `rngFor`; the start, the cast and each bearing roll replay from the seed.
- The authored game (no explicit seed) is identical: same house, same street, same people and spawns.
- Migrations stay append-only: no schema change. Existing suites keep passing except where a test asserted a frozen
  NPC after its agenda, which now follows.

## 7. Out of scope

- Ambient townsfolk walking the streets, and NPC schedules by hour.
- A trailing character that keeps following across two doors in a row.
- Needs, relationships and chained agendas beyond the bearing.
- A larger map than one outdoor zone per district, and vehicles or fast travel.
